DESCRIPTIVE ANALYSIS

Largest daily movements

Largest absolute day-to-day percentage-point change per selected series within the analysis window. Both dates must be present in the window.

A movement is not an explanation

A change in stored volume is not water consumption or pump throughput. Rainfall, inflows, releases, evaporation and operational changes can affect storage. This dataset alone cannot establish their individual contributions. No flood, safety or cybersecurity alert is inferred.

South East Queensland, through its storage data.

FlowGuard is an independent portfolio project by Choshitha Anisetti. It combines Python ingestion, relational data modelling, SQL analysis and an interactive dashboard. It is not affiliated with Seqwater and is not an operational decision system.

Official source

Seqwater historical dam levels. CSV ZIP exported on 21 October 2026; source observations are automated and unverified.

Operating context

The publisher notes reduced full supply levels for North Pine, Somerset and Wivenhoe. A lower percentage alone does not establish a shortage.

What is measured

Storage percentage and stored volume in ML. There are no pump energy, flow, rainfall or demand measurements in this export.
